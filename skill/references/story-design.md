# Story Design

Use before mapping beats or choosing effects.

## Core claim

Find the one sentence the deck must prove:

`For [audience], this shows that [change/stakes] because [evidence].`

If audience, stakes, or desired outcome are missing, ask.

## Sort content

Group material into:
- Context: what readers need before caring.
- Tension: what is surprising, broken, risky, or unresolved.
- Evidence: data, scenes, quotes, examples, documents.
- Mechanism: how it works or why it happened.
- Choice: what the reader should decide, believe, or do.

## Choose angle

Use one primary angle:
- Executive: decision, risk, tradeoffs.
- Investor/product: problem, traction, proof, ask.
- Editorial/report: evidence, stakes, accountability.
- Technical: mechanism, sequence, constraints.
- Learning: concept, example, practice, feedback.

## Interaction rule

Do not add effects to weak content. First clarify claim, stakes, and evidence. Effects should reveal, compare, test, sequence, or connect information.

## Output

Before coding, summarize:
- core claim
- audience
- angle
- beat map
- selected effects and why
